Prepared by: Ravish MaqsoodAll rights reserved. No slide or part to be reproduced or publishedon any platform without permission of the owner. 
Definition
What is Risk?Project Risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if occurs, has a positive or negative effect on one or more project objectives such as scope, schedule, cost and quality.

Prepared by: Ravish MaqsoodAll rights reserved. No slide or part to be reproduced or publishedon any platform without permission of the owner. 
Risks•Risks will continue to emerge through out project lifecycle.•Risks must be monitored and managed.•Important to manage the risks to deliver the project within the expectations of contractual agreement.  •Level of acceptable level of risk exposure to be agreed and known upfront. •Contingencies are built in the cost and schedule estimates for risks.
