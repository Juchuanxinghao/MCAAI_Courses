Enterprise SoftwareHow do most global enterprises deploy their ERP solutions?  •Enterprise resource planning (ERP) is a type of software organizations used to manage day-to-day business activities like supply chain operations, accounting, or project management, among others. •These software solutions can be deployed through different models. •Most companies worldwide report on-premise solutions as their primary method for ERP deployment followed by cloud ERP systems and software-as-a-service solutions. •Over 30 percent of companies worldwide reported cost overruns in their ERP implementation projects as of 2021.
