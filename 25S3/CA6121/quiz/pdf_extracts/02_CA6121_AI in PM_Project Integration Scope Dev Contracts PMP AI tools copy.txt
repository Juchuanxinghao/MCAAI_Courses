Prepared by: Ravish MaqsoodAll rights reserved. No slide or part to be reproduced or publishedon any platform without permission of the owner. 
Develop Project CharterIntroduction– Mention a brief about the project. Project objectives and success criteria–mention project objectives or goals as set by the contract and any specific requirements.Project scope, deliverables and requirements– describe project scope summary, boundaries, and requirements. A detailed deliverable list to be provided as per the contractual agreement. Budget– the cost estimate for the project.Schedule/milestones–project milestones, or stages, for measuring its progress and success. Constraints and assumptions– detail the known and unknown constraints and assumptions. Summary of risks– list all the known possible risk to the project.Team and organization– mention key people and stakeholders with their roles and contact details. Attach an organization chart to show their reporting.Approvals– ensure written endorsed approval from the stakeholder authorized personnel on the project charter document.
