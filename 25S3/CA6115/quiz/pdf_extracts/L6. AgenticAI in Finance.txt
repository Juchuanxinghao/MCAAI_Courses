How does the Agentic AI work?
Page 8
•Perception: Agentic AI uses sensors, data inputs, or APIs to gather information 
from its environment. This could include text, images, numerical data, or real-
time feeds from the web or connected systems. 
•Reasoning and Decision-Making: The AI processes the collected data using 
advanced algorithms, often powered by large language models (LLMs) or 
reinforcement learning. It evaluates options, predicts outcomes, and selects 
actions based on predefined goals or learned objectives. 
• Executes Action: Once a decision is made, the AI executes actions, which 
could involve generating responses, manipulating data, controlling devices, or 
interacting with other systems. 
•Goal-Oriented Behavior: Unlike traditional AI, which might focus on specific 
tasks, agentic AI is driven by broader objectives. It can break down complex 
goals into smaller tasks, prioritize them, and adjust its approach dynamically. 

What technologies do the agents use?
Page 9
•Machine Learning Models: LLMs, neural networks, or 
reinforcement learning frameworks power the AI’s reasoning. 
•APIs and Tools: Agentic AIs often integrate with external systems 
for data access or action execution (e.g., web search, database 
queries). 
•Planning Algorithms: Techniques like hierarchical task networks 
or chain or feedback loops help the AI break down and pursue 
goals. 
•Memory Systems: Short-term and long-term memory (like my 
ability to recall prior conversations) enable continuity and learning 
amongst agents. (simpler for LangChain as objects and states live 
within the key orchestrator)

Hosting your own LLMs
Page 12
• Ollama is a local runtime for large language models (LLMs) that lets 
you run modern open-source models entirely on your own machine (CPU

Model Context Protocol (MCP)
Page 15
• MCP is an open standard that provides a standardized way for large 
language models (LLMs) to connect to external data, tools, and 
services. It acts like a universal adapter, similar to a USB-C port for AI, 
allowing applications to provide LLMs with necessary context to 
perform tasks, build workflows, and interact with the real world more 
effectively.

Case Study: Design Workflow in Agentic AI 
Page 26
• Define Objectives: Clearly state what the agentic workflow needs to 
accomplish.
• Map Current Process: Analyze the existing process to identify tasks and 
potential areas for automation.
• What patterns are appropriate? 
• Determine the types of processes encompass: perception, action, supervision 
in each agent?
• What memory is needed? To pass between agents?  
• What is the knowledge base? Data input.. Either dynamic (by MCP) or static?
• What LLM would you use? Is it for coding? Or writing? Etc. 
• What type of actions are needed? Send emails/ reports? 
• What type of supervision? Human in the loop?

Conclusion
Page 28
Concept & Evolution:
Agentic AI refers to autonomous, goal-directed systems that perceive, reason, and act 
using data, APIs, and planning algorithms that moves from rule-based automation to 
adaptive, intelligent decision-making.
Enterprise & Technical Foundations:
Enterprise-ready agentic systems combine LLMs, machine-learning models, APIs, 
memory, and orchestration tools while ensuring scalability, integration, and security 
within corporate environments.
Workflow & Design Patterns:
Common patterns include delegation/hand-off, tool-use and function-calling, 
sequential chains, judge-and-critic feedback loops, parallelisation for speed, and 
guardrails for safety, governance, and privacy.
Finance Applications & Case Workflows:
Demonstrated through corporate FX hedging and stock-trading agents, the slides 
emphasise designing workflows that define objectives, map processes, choose agent 
types, set memory and knowledge bases, specify actions and determine appropriate 
human supervision.
