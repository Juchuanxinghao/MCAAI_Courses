Topic Classification
• Supervised learning method to classify text into its 
relevant topics. The idea is that common topics tend to 
have common words. For eg. In finance, lingo like interest 
rates, trading, options, derivatives are common.
• The machine learns these words from a reference text, 
and then apply to new texts to classify them. In finance, 
category of news, IPO, compliance, bond raising etc.

What is technical analysis?
27
• Technical analysis is a method of predicting the future 
direction of a market’s price by studying historical chart 
patterns and formations. 
• The aim of technical analysis is to identify recognisable 
patterns to help traders find the right time and price point 
at which to enter and exit the market.

Primary Trends in Dow Theory
30
Accumulation Phase: (consolidation)
•Occurs after a substantial decline in prices.
•Typically marked by negative sentiment, 
bearish media coverage, and frantic selling 
by uninformed participants.
•Informed investors take advantage of low 
prices to accumulate shares gradually, 
anticipating a bullish reversal.
Trend Phase:
•Triggered by expectations of higher prices post-
accumulation or lower prices post-distribution.
•Begins with technical breakouts signaling an 
uptrend, attracting early adopters and savvy 
investors.
•Media coverage becomes optimistic, drawing 
increased public participation.
•Participants exhibit regret bias, buying on dips and 
covering short positions as prices rise.
Distribution Phase:
•Follows a significant price increase, 
accompanied by positive company data and 
bullish media coverage.
•Uninformed participants exhibit bullish 
sentiment, leading to a buying frenzy and rising 
margin debt.
•Informed investors initiate selling campaigns.

Moving Averages

35
• MACD is a technical analysis indicator used to identify the momentum and 
direction of a trend. 
• Comprises 2 technical indicators
• MACD line = 12 period EWMA - 26-period EMA 
• Signal line = 9-period EMA of this MACD line  
• Stages:
• Convergence (crossover): MACD line crosses above the signal line, it's 
considered a buy signal. When it crosses below, it's a sell signal
• Divergence: When the MACD line diverges from the price, it can signal a 
potential reversal in the market.
• There is no reason to conform to the parameters set.

Indicators & Oscillators: Bollinger bands 
40
• Determine oversold or overbought conditions. Indication of volatility.

Indicators & Oscillators: Bollinger bands  
41
• Some ‘words of wisdom’
• If the band tightens, possibility of sharp up or down movements 
(trend)
• If the band widens, existing trend could lead to consolidation. (ending 
trend)
• If prices exceeds upper band -> sign of overbought
• If prices breaks lower band -> sign of oversold
• These ‘wisdom rules’ can be written as part of predictors for ML 
algorithms.

Conclusion
• From NLP to LLMs:
Natural Language Processing (NLP) converts unstructured text into 
numerical representations (tokens, word vectors, embeddings) that allow 
machines to understand language contextually, paving the way for modern 
Large Language Models (LLMs).
• Customization and Future Trends:
Enterprise-grade solutions use RAG and MCP to integrate LLMs with 
proprietary datasets and tools, addressing the limitations of generic models 
— though many GenAI pilots still struggle due to poor integration and 
governance.
