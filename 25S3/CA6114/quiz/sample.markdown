## Part 1 练习题

### 1. Which of the following best describes Generative AI?

A. It only predicts numerical values from historical data
B. It learns patterns from data and generates new content
C. It only classifies images into fixed categories
D. It is another name for traditional rule-based systems

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
Generative AI 的核心是从数据中学习模式，然后 **生成新的内容** ，而不是只做分类或预测。/ Generative AI learns patterns from data and creates new content, rather than only classifying or predicting.

---

### 2. Which of the following is the most accurate comparison between Generative AI and Predictive AI?

A. Generative AI predicts labels, while Predictive AI generates images
B. Generative AI creates new outputs, while Predictive AI focuses on forecasting or classification
C. They are identical in function
D. Predictive AI is only used for text tasks

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
这是高频基础题。GenAI 主要是“生成”，Predictive AI 主要是“预测/分类”。/ GenAI focuses on creation, while Predictive AI focuses on prediction or classification.

---

### 3. Which of the following is most suitable for Generative AI?

A. Strict tax rule calculation with no tolerance for error
B. Drafting multiple marketing slogans
C. Final legal judgment with perfect factual accuracy required
D. Safety-critical control in medical equipment

**Answer: B** 

**解析：**
营销文案生成属于创造性、变化性强、可多版本输出的任务，最适合 GenAI。其余选项都对确定性、准确性、可审计性要求极高。/ Marketing slogan generation is a creative, variable task suited to GenAI, unlike strict or safety-critical tasks.

---

### 4. Which of the following is a key concern in AI readiness?

A. Whether the organisation has enough governance, capability, and support for AI adoption
B. Whether employees like science fiction movies
C. Whether the organisation uses only cloud storage
D. Whether all staff can code in Python

**Answer: A**

**解析：**
AI readiness 关注的是组织是否在治理、能力、流程和技术上准备好引入 AI。/ AI readiness concerns whether the organisation is prepared in governance, capability, process, and technology.

---

### 5. Which statement about GenAI ethics is most correct?

A. Bias is no longer a concern in GenAI systems
B. GenAI can amplify bias already present in training data
C. Ethics only matters after deployment
D. Ethical concerns are separate from governance

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
课件明确提到 biased outputs 是 GenAI 的核心风险之一，生成模型会继承并放大训练数据偏见。/ GenAI can inherit and amplify biases from its training data.

### 6. Which of the following best explains why deepfakes are considered dangerous?

A. They are always easy to detect
B. They can mislead people and undermine trust, governance, and accountability
C. They only affect entertainment content
D. They are unrelated to GenAI governance

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
deepfake 的危险在于它会误导公众、破坏信任，并引发治理与问责问题。/ Deepfakes are dangerous because they can mislead people and damage trust, governance, and accountability.

### 8. Which of the following are common GenAI governance concerns? Select all that apply.

B. Transparency
C. Accountability

### 9. A company uses GenAI to generate customer-facing responses without human review. Which risk is most relevant?

A. Hallucination and inappropriate output
B. Better explainability
C. Guaranteed compliance
D. Reduced need for governance

**Answer: A**

**解析：**
如果没有人工审核，GenAI 可能 hallucinate，或者输出不合适内容，尤其是直接面对客户时风险更高。/ Without human review, GenAI may hallucinate or produce inappropriate customer-facing outputs.

### 10. Which of the following use cases is LEAST appropriate for GenAI?

A. Brainstorming rough ideas
B. Creating draft content for internal review
C. Producing multiple creative alternatives
D. Making a final high-stakes decision that requires perfect reliability

**Answer: D**

**解析：**
高风险、零容错、必须完全可靠的最终决策不适合直接交给 GenAI。/ Final high-stakes decisions requiring perfect reliability are not well-suited to GenAI.

### 12. Which statement best explains why AI security is not the same as traditional software security?

A. AI security ignores software vulnerabilities
B. AI systems introduce additional attack surfaces such as prompts, models, and data pipelines
C. Traditional software security is no longer needed
D. AI security only concerns hardware

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
AI security 包含传统安全问题，但还新增模型、提示词、训练数据、推理链路等攻击面。/ AI security includes traditional software issues plus new attack surfaces like prompts, models, and data pipelines.

### 13. Which of the following is one of the three Secure by Design principles?

A. Eliminate all transparency
B. Lead from the Top
C. Hide all incidents permanently
D. Remove all security ownership

**Answer: B**

**解析：**
Secure by Design 的三原则之一就是  **Lead from the Top** 。/ “Lead from the Top” is one of the core Secure by Design principles.

### 14. Which of the following are the four generic secure design principles mentioned in the slides? Select all that apply.

A. Raise staff awareness of threats and risks
B. Model the threats to your system
C. Design for security as well as functionality and performance
D. Consider security benefits and trade-offs when selecting your AI model
E. Always choose the largest possible model

**Answer: A, B, C, D**

**解析：**
A–D 都是课件中的 secure design principles。E 不对，因为模型选择要看 trade-off，不是越大越好。/ A–D are the listed secure design principles; model choice should be based on trade-offs, not size alone.
