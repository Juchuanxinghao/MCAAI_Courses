**Which of the SSDF practice groups target to have software with minimal security vulnerabilities in its releases?**

A. Prepare the Organization (PO)
B. Protect the Software (PS)
C. Produce Well-Secured Software (PW)
D. Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV)

**C. Produce Well-Secured Software (PW)**

**In Table 1 of NIST SP 800-218A, what information is primarily provided in the “Practice” column?**

A. The list of tasks an organization should do.
B. The name of the security practice, its unique identifier, and a brief explanation of its purpose.
C. A ranking of AI risks associated with the system.
D. A list of tools required to implement the practice.



**B. The name of the security practice, its unique identifier, and a brief explanation of its purpose.**

解析：NIST 原文说明：**Practice contains the name of the practice and a unique identifier, followed by a brief explanation of what the practice is and why it is beneficial.** 这和 B 一致。

**In Table 1 of NIST SP 800-218A, if the “Recommendations, Considerations, and Notes” column states “No additions to SSDF 1.1”, what does this imply?**

A. The task is no longer relevant to AI systems.
B. The profile replaces the SSDF guidance entirely.
C. The task only applies to legacy systems.
D. Organizations should refer to the baseline SSDF guidance for the task.



**D. Organizations should refer to the baseline SSDF guidance for the task.**

解析：218A 是在 SSDF 1.1 之上做补充；如果某项写的是  **No additions to SSDF 1.1** ，意思就是这项没有额外 AI-specific 补充，按原本 **SSDF 1.1 baseline guidance** 去看即可。
