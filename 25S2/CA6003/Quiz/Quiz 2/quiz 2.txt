Details of the Quiz

There will be around 25-30 questions in the quiz. Total point is 45. Marks for questions may be different.
The questions will be MCQs, Fill-in-the-Blanks, Multiple Answers, Matching questions etc., that is, objective (auto-graded).
Negative marking for wrong answers for multiple answer multichoice questions.
If there is any confusion regarding the correct answer(s), choose option(s) that seem most appropriate.
The quiz will use Respondus Lockdown Browser.
Remember to "Save and Submit" the quiz, once done. You will see timer and the relevant warnings.
Let the Lab In-Charge and/or the Instructor/TA know if you face any technical problem whatsoever!
